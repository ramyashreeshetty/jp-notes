  1. 20 September 2026Sunday

20 September 2026九月二十日 日曜日

Dates, weather, doko ni mo with a negative, tame ni for purpose, nakute wa narimasen.

The entry

Ayano-sensei, konnichiwa.
Hello, Ayano-sensei.
あやの先生、こんにちは。
Kore wa hajimete no nikki desu.
This is my first diary.
これは はじめての 日記です。
Kyou wa kugatsu hatsuka, nichiyoubi desu.
Today is Sunday, 20 September.
きょうは 九月二十日、日曜日です。
Soshite, ame desu.
And it is raining.
そして、雨です。
Kyou wa doko ni mo ikimasen.
Today I am not going anywhere.
きょうは どこにも 行きません。
Demo kinou wa Resident Evil no eiga o miru tame ni eigakan ni ikimashita.
But yesterday I went to the cinema to watch the Resident Evil film.
でも きのうは レジデント・イービルの 映画を 見るために 映画館に 行きました。
Sorekara, nihongo o benkyou shimashita.
After that, I studied Japanese.
それから、日本語を 勉強しました。
Nihongo de hanasu koto wa hazukashii desu.
Speaking in Japanese makes me shy.
日本語で 話すことは はずかしいです。
Nihongo wa muzukashii desu ne.
Japanese is difficult, isn't it.
日本語は むずかしいですね。
Demo hanasanakute wa narimasen.
But I have to speak it.
でも 話さなくては なりません。
Ashita wa nihongo no kurasu ga arimasu.
Tomorrow I have Japanese class.
あしたは 日本語の クラスが あります。
Kurasu de aimasu ne.
I will see you in class.
クラスで 会いますね。
Arigatou gozaimasu. Mata ashita.
Thank you. See you tomorrow.
ありがとうございます。また あした。

Corrected before recording

SaidCorrectRule
hazukashi, muzukashihazukashii, muzukashiiThe doubled vowel belongs to the word. i-adjectives end in it: oishii, tanoshii, muzukashii.
hanasu kucha na kuchahanasanakute wa narimasenBuilt on the nai form, never the dictionary form. hanasu to hanasanai to hanasanakute.
watashi wa nihongo de hanasu koto wanihongo de hanasu koto waOne wa to a sentence. Once koto wa is the topic, watashi wa has no work left.
tokoro ni ikimasendoko ni mo ikimasenNot anywhere is doko ni mo with a negative verb, the same shape as nani mo and dare mo.
shichigatsukugatsuSeptember is the ninth month. shichigatsu is July.
nijuu nichihatsukaThe 20th is irregular, as are the 1st to the 10th, the 14th and the 24th.
hajimete koe nikkihajimete no nikkihajimete takes no in front of a noun. Bare hajimete goes in front of a verb.
eigakan o ikimashitaeigakan ni ikimashitaA destination takes ni or e. o marks an object, and a cinema is not one.
miru tame ni o ikimashitamiru tame ni ikimashitaNothing follows tame ni. The main verb comes straight after it.
kurasu ni aimasukurasu de aimasuni marks the person met. The place the meeting happens in takes de.
hajimete and hajimete no

In front of a verb it stands alone: hajimete mimashita, I saw it for the first time. In front of a noun it takes no: hajimete no nikki, my first diary.
