# Verbs

The 55 N5 verbs from `notes/reference/n5-verb-list.xlsx`, in three tiers.

- **Practised** — a sentence of her own exists in the verb sheet
- **Taught** — the verb appears in a class note, but she has not built a sentence with it
- **Untouched** — neither
