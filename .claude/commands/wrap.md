Catch-all for learning that happened in conversation rather than through
`/ingest` or `/test` — a word looked up, a pattern explained, a correction made
in passing.

1. Look back over this session for anything Japanese that was taught, corrected,
   or struggled with and isn't yet on disk.
2. Add new items to `brain/` at L0. Add corrections to `log/mistakes.md`.
3. Append a row to `log/sessions.md` describing what the session covered.
4. Regenerate `STATUS.md`.
5. List what was written. If nothing new came up, say so — don't manufacture
   entries to look productive.
